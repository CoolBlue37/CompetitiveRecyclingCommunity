Leaderboard

About the Project

What is this?

The Recycling Leaderboard turns recycling into a friendly competition between grades.

How it works

  1. Drop a recyclable in the bin.
  2. Press your grade's button (9th, 10th, 11th, or 12th).
  3. The bin checks whether it went in the right place.
  4. The bin detects what kind of item it was: organic, glass, miscellaneous, paper, cardboard, plastic, or metal. Each grade's count for that category goes up by one.
  5. Your grade's score is the percentage of items sorted correctly (correct ÷ total), so wrong bins lower it.

Why?

At SAS, we have realized that people do not recycle trash at all. Only 52% of waste in Singapore is recycled, so this project helps increase the amount of recycling in the community.
