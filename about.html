About the Project

What is this?

The Recycling Leaderboard turns recycling into a friendly competition between grades.

How it works

  1. Drop a recyclable in the bin.
  2. Press your grade's button (9th, 10th, 11th, or 12th).
  3. Watch your grade's bar grow on the leaderboard.

Why?

At SAS, we have realized that people do not recycle trash at all. Only 52% of waste in singapore is recycled, hence this projects helps increase the amount of recycling in the community.
